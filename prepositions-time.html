Prepositions of Time

Prepositions of time: in / on / at

PrepositionUseExample
inmonths, seasons, years, parts of dayin January / summer / the morning
ondays, dateson Monday / 5th May / the weekend
atclock time, night, noonat 7 o’clock / noon / night

Time adverbs — NO preposition! Type “–” or leave empty

ExpressionExample
yesterday / yesterday morningI saw her yesterday.
last night / last week / last yearWe met last Saturday.
this morning / this afternoon / this weekHe called this morning.
... ago (two days ago, a year ago)She arrived three days ago.

Prepositions of Place

Prepositions of place

PrepositionMeaningExample
ininside a spaceShe is in the office.
onon a surfaceThe book is on the table.
atat a specific point / placeHe is at home. She is at work.
abovehigher, not touchingThe lamp is above the table.
underbelow, lower thanThe cat is under the chair.
behindat the back ofThe park is behind the school.
in front offacing, at the front ofThere is a bus stop in front of the hotel.
acrosson the other side ofThe shop is across the street.
